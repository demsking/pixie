Simple tools for everyday image edits.

Pixie keeps the common image tasks close at hand, with clear tools that do exactly what you expect.

Crop

Trim away unwanted edges and keep the part of the picture that matters.

Resize

Make images smaller or fit them to the size you need for sharing, posting, or saving space.

Clean Metadata

Remove hidden details like location, camera, and editing information before you share an image.

Edit Metadata

Review and update image details when you want the file to carry the right information.

Transparent Background

Remove a background so the subject can stand on its own in designs, documents, and profile images.

Rotate

Turn sideways or upside-down photos into the correct orientation in a single step.

Mirror Image

Reverse a picture left to right when it looks better facing the other way.

Flip

Flip an image from top to bottom to quickly correct or experiment with its direction.

Convert Images

Save an image in another format when you need a different file type for a website, app, or device.
